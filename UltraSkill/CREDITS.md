## 1. Package License & Authorship

**UltraSkill v1.0.0** consists of original documentation, templates, schemas, and scripts written specifically for this package. No proprietary assets, copyrighted characters, commercial 3D scans, or trademarked logos are included.

## 2. Structural Inspiration

The modular studio organization of UltraSkill—using a lightweight top-level router, numbered skill folders with dedicated `references/`, `scripts/`, and `templates/`, an iterate-in-rounds studio quality loop, written measurable quality bars, a round ledger, side-by-side comparison sheets, and a fake-3D diagnostic checklist—draws structural inspiration from **Toggle Studio** (`toggleops.com/op/toggle-studio`). All prose, numeric tables, checklists, templates, and executable code in UltraSkill are original work authored from scratch.
